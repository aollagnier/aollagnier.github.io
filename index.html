01 / ABOUT

Language, people
& their interactions.

I am an Assistant Professor in Computer Science at Université Côte d’Azur, conducting research at the I3S laboratory in Sophia Antipolis within the MARIANNE team.

My research brings together machine learning, natural language processing and high-level linguistic analysis to understand online interactions, with a particular focus on cyberbullying detection and prevention, online hate and dialogue modeling.

Previously, I was a Junior Fellow in AI at 3IA Côte d’Azur and a postdoctoral researcher at Université Côte d’Azur and the University of Exeter. I earned my PhD in Computer Science at Aix-Marseille Université in 2017.
